TG ESP32-C3 Zero Protective Case

We have designed a case, named the TG case, to hold the ESP32-C3 Zero micro-controller board during normal class use by IT students

  • A case to protect the ESP32-C3 Zero during classroom use, allowing it to be plugged in (USB-C port) & have the pins protected & accessible whilst inside the case
  • The main features of this case are durability & accessibility to the ports/buttons
  • Used by (presumably IT) students in a classroom with the aim of reducing breakages & fragility
