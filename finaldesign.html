Final Design

Final design of the TG case, incorporating improvements from previous prototypes with further improved button accesss

Final design features and benefits

The final design brings together the improvements made across both prototypes into a single, refined case. Ventilation holes replace the earlier slot design for better airflow, and the button openings have been resized so the BOOT and RESET buttons can be pressed without resistance from the case. Full access to the pins, buttons, and USB-C port has been maintained throughout, meeting all the criteria required for a suitable ESP32-C3 Zero case.

Feature Details
Ventilation and button access Ventilation holes, modified button access.
Button accessibility Buttons are now easily accessible without resistence from the case. Ventilation slots changed to holes improve airflow.
Design criteria fulfilment This design fulfils all criteria required for a suitable ESP32-C3 Zero case, allowing access to the pins, BOOT & RESET buttons & USB-C port.
